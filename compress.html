Compression Levels Explained

Low Compression

Lightest settings with higher image quality. Best when you want a gentle size reduction.

Medium Compression (Recommended)

Balanced settings for most documents. Structural optimization is preferred when it wins.

High Compression

Most aggressive image-based settings. Best for scanned PDFs and upload limits.

Target Size

Multiple compression versions are generated and the best-quality result under your target size is chosen whenever possible.
